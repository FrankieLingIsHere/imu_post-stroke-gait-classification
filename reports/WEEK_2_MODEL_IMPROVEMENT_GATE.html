Week 2: Model Improvement Gate

Current development-selected lower-back candidate, evaluated with source-disjoint evidence.

Decision: Admit the fixed equal-probability ERM + Deep CORAL + ERM++-style ensemble as the development-selected lower-back candidate. This is not an external or clinical validation claim.

Protocol

  • 314 participants and 22,506 real windows from Felius, Voisard, and Sint Maartenskliniek.
  • One 500-sample lower-back acceleration-magnitude channel.
  • Leave-one-complete-source-out evaluation, five seeds, participant-level metrics.
  • RevalExo and NONAN were not used for training, tuning, threshold selection, or acceptance.
