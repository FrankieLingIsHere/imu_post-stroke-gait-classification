# Data sufficiency decision

The current data is sufficient to continue development of a research prototype and source-level robustness benchmarking. It is not sufficient for reliable age/sex subgroup claims, stroke-specific clinical discrimination, or clinical-ready deployment claims.

The next data acquisition priority is clinical specificity and subject-level metadata, not simply increasing the number of overlapping windows. Mobilise-D is the strongest public candidate for non-stroke clinical specificity, but its released data format must be audited before any pooling decision. Zhou remains a stroke-only severity/longitudinal resource, while the triaxial healthy dataset remains an age/domain or self-supervised resource because its channel layout is incompatible with the current classifier.
