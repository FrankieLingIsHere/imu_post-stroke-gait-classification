Decision

The fixed MiniROCKET/logistic fusion was rejected because it increased total errors, worsened AUROC and Brier score, and did not transport consistently across held-out sources. The admitted deep ensemble remains the development model.

Next gate

Participant-level multiple-instance learning was tested as a distinct label-granularity hypothesis. If it does not improve the predeclared false-positive and false-negative gates, the limiting factor should be recorded as cohort and label overlap rather than pursued through repeated threshold searches.
