## Curation policy

Keep only interpretation-ready figures, tables, and concise decision records here. Download logs, transient console output, and superseded rendering artefacts belong in `reports/archive/` and are excluded from public version control. Each report must identify its notebook or script evidence source and must not imply clinical validation.
