Week 2: Cross-Dataset Evidence Gate

Mandatory discipline for every future method, enrichment attempt, and external evaluation.

Purpose: Keep promising-looking improvements from becoming source-specific or leakage-prone claims.
Official implementation checkTrace the published code before re-implementing a method.
Matched baselineCompare every candidate with an ERM control under the same protocol.
Participant/source separationUse complete-source and participant-disjoint validation.
Training-only fittingFit normalization and generators on training data only; keep validation and testing real.
Worst-source reportingReport source-wise clinical metrics, not only pooled means.
Frozen external cohortsNever use RevalExo or NONAN for model selection, threshold, or calibration.

Why prior negatives may differ from literature

Many published successes concern generic activity recognition or gait detection rather than disease classification. Others use target-domain data during fitting, while this project forbids that. Synthetic gains can also reverse for individual held-out people or larger configurations.
