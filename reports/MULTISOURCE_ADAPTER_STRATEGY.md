# Multi-source adapter strategy

The five healthy datasets are not forced into the canonical three-channel format. Each adapter records observed body locations and a channel mask. Complete observed Waist/LF/RF cycles are eligible for canonical healthy synthesis. DUO-GAIT's OG_st_control release has complete sacrum/LF/RF files for its 16 participants; sacrum is retained explicitly as a trunk proxy, not relabelled as lumbar without validation. Partial sources may support masked-channel encoder pretraining, but reconstructed channels are not treated as measurements. Hip/wrist sources remain domain-pretraining or separate-test data. Unparseable sources remain blocked.

The registry is generated by `scripts/build_multisource_adapter_registry.py` and stored in `data/processed/multisource_adapter_registry.csv`.

## Model route

`source adapter → observed-channel mask → shared temporal encoder → conditional full-cycle decoder`.

The decoder may reconstruct missing channels as an auxiliary task, but a sample is admitted to canonical classifier training only when the three channels are genuinely observed or validated through paired recordings. This prevents technical reshaping from being mistaken for biomechanical equivalence.
