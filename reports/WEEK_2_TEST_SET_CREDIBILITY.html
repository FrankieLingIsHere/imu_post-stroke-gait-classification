Planning illustration

At current development rates, approximately 257 independent healthy participants and 222 independent stroke participants would be needed for two-sided 95% Wilson intervals no wider than 10 percentage points. This is a planning illustration, not a universal regulatory requirement.

Required final-test safeguards

  • Freeze architecture, preprocessing, ensemble, threshold, and exclusions before access.
  • Use participants absent from training, tuning, representation choice, and threshold selection.
  • Report participant-level TN, FP, FN, TP, sensitivity, specificity, AUROC, calibration, and intervals.
  • Do not update the model after opening the final test.
