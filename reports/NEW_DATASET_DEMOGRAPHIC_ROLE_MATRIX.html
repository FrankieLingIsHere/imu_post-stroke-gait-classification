New Dataset Demographic Role Matrix

What each recruited public source can and cannot contribute to demographic validation.

Answer: The new datasets are useful, but they do not collectively provide a complete age-stratified stroke-versus-healthy test. Sint improves compatible model robustness; Zhou improves stroke severity evidence; Triaxial improves older-healthy coverage; Mobilise-D improves clinical specificity; Voisard provides wider age context. A balanced age-labelled stroke and healthy source is still missing.
