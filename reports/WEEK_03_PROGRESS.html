Gait-cycle consistency implementation

Five tests passed for coupled speed, cadence, step lengths and contact/support timing. The tests verify algebraic consistency, not clinical phenotype realism.

Six-channel virtual IMU pilot

18 healthy GAITEX participants, 55 windows, 500 samples and six channels per window. The calculation includes sensor orientation, gravity and attachment offsets. Six physics tests passed, covering gravity, free fall, rotating attachment acceleration, angular acceleration, frame invariance and invalid inputs.

The pilot used existing motion recordings, taking the first valid run per annotated normal segment. One of 19 inventoried parent directories lacked required source files and was recorded in coverage. Parent development roles were assigned before generation: 14 available build participants and four holdout participants. Output shape, finite values, index mapping and parent-role checks passed.

Signals are expressed in a pelvis marker frame, not validated L5 or hardware IMU axes. The pilot is a completed healthy sensor-generation component; it does not demonstrate a stroke transformation, clinical validation or improved classifier performance. No new classifier training occurred.

What the current models actually are

Deep-learning research reference

Six-channel stride CNN

  1. Lower-back acceleration XYZ + gyroscope XYZReference-assisted straight strides

1,058 trainable parameters. Same architecture in the earlier VGA and latest regularization runs. Trained from scratch; no ElderNet fusion, recurrent block or attention.

Pinned Linn39 builder: 689cc9b. Fold/seed checkpoints are evaluation models, not an automatically validated deployment ensemble.

Packaged stroke research prototype

Ten-feature logistic regression

  1. Participant feature rowAge, device fraction, protocol length and cadence
  2. Phase and harmonic-ratio featuresStep/swing/stance asymmetry, swing fraction, double support, nominal-Y log HR
  3. Median imputation + missingness indicatorOnly age may be missing at the input API
  4. StandardScaler → logistic regressionC = 1 · maximum 3,000 iterations
  5. Research score and thresholdInvalid inputs retained as unknown

This model is not deep learning. Explicit age, device and protocol covariates prevent interpreting its performance as a pure IMU physiological signature.

Package: stroke-phase-hr-v0.1.0. Its full-data threshold .3953767334 was chosen after development inspection and is not the threshold used for the table above.

Neither model is autonomous raw-IMU clinical inference. The CNN still needs reference-assisted segmentation; the feature model needs annotation-assisted phase measurements. The waveform frame is not validated anatomical calibration.

06 / Interpretation

What these results establish

The reported comparisons are completed development evaluations. No unique biological cause of false positives was established, and no model was promoted from the regularization comparison. DUO-GAIT has no VGA labels, so its alerts are not verified VGA false positives. Repeated development folds are not an untouched final test.

Metadata and labels were documented; existing raw signals were reused; the reported preprocessing and evaluations were executed. These research results do not establish clinical diagnostic validity.
