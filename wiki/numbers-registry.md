## Round-16 code-level fixes behind the numbers above (see `log.md` for full narrative)

- **M1 (fatal, confirmed via primary source)**: DUO-GAIT's raw `repository_raw/OG_st_raw` files are the ENTIRE ~49-minute visit (6-min control walk + non-walking fatigue protocol + 6-min fatigue walk concatenated), not a walking trial — confirmed against Zhou et al. (2023), PMC10442385. Fixed by switching to the dataset's own already-segmented `repository_interim/OG_st_control` walking-only files.
- **M2**: MAREA and Camargo (already properly windowed, unlike DUO-GAIT) shared DUO-GAIT's step/stride harmonic-ambiguity bug in `windowed_stride_time_cv`. Extended `disambiguate_harmonic=True` to both. Also found and fixed an independent bug in the disambiguation logic itself: the accepted half-lag candidate wasn't bounds-checked against `min_lag`, producing a few implausible cadences (~260-286 steps/min) for Camargo — fixed with a `min_lag` floor.
- **M3**: `sample_entropy()`'s old fixed-sample-count cap (1500) created a ~20-30x difference in effective temporal resolution across datasets depending on trial duration (DUO-GAIT/MAREA ~4 Hz effective vs. Camargo ~93 Hz). Fixed by resampling every signal to a common 10 Hz effective rate before computing entropy.
- **M4**: Voisard's Section 4.2.2 age-adjustment switched from trial level to participant level, consistent with the review's own stated principle (already applied in Section 4.2.6).
- **M6**: P154's SNR ranking recomputed with the correct 9 candidates (foot RMS included) and after the M3 fix.

## Round-18 code-level fixes behind the numbers above (see `log.md` for full narrative)

GaitMotion's own SD1 value is now excluded (NaN) rather than computed on an unconfirmed rate, consistent with its cadence/harmonic-ratio treatment.

Notebook fixed to cite the current 17.5% figure and to clarify it affects foot signal-derived timing features specifically, not foot accelerometer RMS.
