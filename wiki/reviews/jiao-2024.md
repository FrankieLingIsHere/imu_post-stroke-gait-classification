Jiao, Y., Hart, R., Reading, S., & Zhang, Y. (2024), *Systematic review of automatic post-stroke gait classification systems*, *Gait and Posture* 109:259–270. The only systematic review to date focused specifically on this class of system, and the closest existing precedent to this whole project.

## What it covers

Searched PubMed, IEEE Xplore, and Scopus in March 2022, narrowing 407 records to 21 included studies. Splits them three ways by what they classify: 10 stroke-vs-healthy, 3 stroke-vs-other-condition (including [[mannini-2016]] and [[hsu-2018]]), 8 stroke-subgroup-only (including [[wang-2021]]).

## The two findings this review builds on directly

- **Six methodological quality criteria**, scored against all 21 studies: no single study met all six; only one (a 2021 EEG-based classifier) met five. Sampling was the weakest criterion — only [[hsu-2018]] avoided within-participant train/test leakage. This finding is the direct template for this review's own nested-CV protocol (see the manuscript's Section 3.8) and for [[quality-assessment]]'s own rubric.
- **Single-label vs. multi-label limitation**: 10 of 13 comparison studies use binary single-label classification, of limited clinical value since a single label can't indicate which specific gait deviation is present. Only [[wang-2021]] connects its output to specific joint deviations (drop foot, circumduction, hip hiking, back knee).
