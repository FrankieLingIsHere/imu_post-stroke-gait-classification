da Silva, R.S. et al. (2024), *Psychometric properties of wearable technologies to assess post-stroke gait parameters: A systematic review*, *Gait and Posture* 113:543–552. Interrogates a question the classification literature usually just assumes: is the underlying sensor measurement itself trustworthy?

## What it covers

42 studies: 37 addressing validity, 16 reliability, 6 measurement error. Finds good reliability for gait speed and step count (certainty rated low), indeterminate measurement-error evidence. Only 2 of 42 studies assess angular kinematic changes — a substantial gap given how central joint-angle information is to clinical gait assessment.
