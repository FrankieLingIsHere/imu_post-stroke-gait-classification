# Research wiki

This Obsidian-compatible knowledge base records dataset facts, research decisions, method notes, literature context, and a chronological change log.

- `datasets/` — source-specific structure, eligibility, role, and split notes.
- `concepts/` — methodological and clinical concepts.
- `studies/` — evidence notes for individual papers.
- `reviews/` — systematic-review material.
- `log.md` — chronological decision and execution record.

The wiki is explanatory documentation, not an alternative source of metrics. Reported model numbers must remain traceable to an executed notebook or a committed report artefact. Do not add participant-level metadata, access-controlled source files, or local filesystem paths.
