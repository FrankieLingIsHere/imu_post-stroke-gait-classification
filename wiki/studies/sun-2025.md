Sun et al. (2025), *IMU-based quantitative assessment of stroke from gait*, *Scientific Reports* 15:9541. Predicts lower-limb Fugl-Meyer Assessment score directly from gait features on the best-performing placement configuration (hip + both thighs).

## Key findings

- Reports an adjusted R-squared of 0.999 — a near-perfect fit.
- **[[quality-assessment]] flags this as a real overfitting risk**: no train/test split or cross-validation is described anywhere in the methods. The near-perfect R² on only 16 participants is best read as a methodological red flag, not a validated result.
- Chronicity 5.75 ± 7 weeks (subacute) and Fugl-Meyer LE 21.56 ± 6.3 — one of only two studies (with [[inui-2026]]) reporting both a numeric chronicity value and a standardized severity score together.
- Data available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.
