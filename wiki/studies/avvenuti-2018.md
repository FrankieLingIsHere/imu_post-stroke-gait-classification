Avvenuti et al. (2018), *Smart shoe-assisted evaluation of using a single trunk or pocket-worn accelerometer to detect gait phases*, *Sensors* 18(11):3811. The first study admitted through [[eligibility-criteria]]'s IC5 pathway — no stroke population and no trained classifier at all, but a directly relevant trunk-vs-pocket placement comparison.

## Key findings

- Mean absolute error below 30ms for stride time at both placements, below 50ms for stance/swing duration.
- Validated against ground truth from pressure-instrumented smart shoes plus video verification.
- The only included study to test trunk-vs-pocket placement head-to-head with no classification task at all — isolating the placement-practicality question from the classification-accuracy question the rest of [[sensor-placement]]'s evidence addresses.
- Exempt from [[quality-assessment]] by design (IC5-admitted, no trained classifier, no stroke population).
