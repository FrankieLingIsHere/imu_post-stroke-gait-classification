Shin (2022), *Deep convolutional neural network-based hemiplegic gait detection using an inertial sensor located freely in a pocket*, *Sensors* 22(5):1920. One of two included studies (with [[lee-2025]]) testing a pocket-carried placement directly in a real stroke cohort — the core evidence behind [[placement-vs-practicality]].

## Key findings

- 0.78 accuracy, 0.80 precision, 0.80 recall — lower than the fixed-placement studies, but confirms pocket placement is usable, not absent, for stroke-specific gait assessment.
- [[quality-assessment]]: held-out/CV confirmed (leave-one-out), no code/data statement, neither chronicity nor severity reported. One of only two included studies (with [[inui-2026]]) reporting an explicit statistical comparability check between groups (age/sex not significantly different, p > 0.05).
