Lee et al. (2025), *Determination of spatiotemporal gait parameters using a smartphone's IMU in the pocket: Threshold-based and deep learning approaches*, *Sensors* 25(14):4395. Pushes furthest toward consumer hardware of any included study — an unmodified smartphone's own sensor, not a dedicated wearable, compressed via knowledge distillation for on-device deployment.

## Key findings

- Gait-event-timing error below 1% for healthy gait, up to approximately 4% for stroke gait.
- The other half of [[placement-vs-practicality]]'s core evidence, alongside [[shin-2022]].
- [[quality-assessment]]: held-out/CV confirmed (true held-out split, not just CV), data available on request, chronicity reported as a wide range (8–287 months, predominantly chronic) and FAC per patient — both reported. Healthy group age 23.8y vs. stroke 60.7y, a roughly 37-year gap, the starkest unaddressed age gap of any included study.
