Wu et al. (2025), *Development of an IMU-based post-stroke gait data acquisition and analysis system for the Gait Assessment and Intervention Tool*, *Sensors* 25(7):1994. Like [[felius-2024]], this was already discussed extensively in the review's Related Work before a supplementary pass formally added it to Table 3.

## Key findings

- RMSE 5.42cm (position accuracy). Pearson r = 0.752, Spearman rho = 0.933 against physician G.A.I.T. score ratings, both p < .01 — evidence deep learning can reproduce a clinician's graded judgment, not only a binary diagnosis.
- Applies deep learning to a severity-scoring regression task rather than classification — its correlation figure sits outside the 78–100% accuracy range [[classification-methods]] reports for the classification-task studies.
- [[quality-assessment]]: held-out/CV confirmed (80/20 split, 96/24 entries), **no code/data statement — data explicitly restricted** ("not readily available... part of an ongoing study"), neither chronicity nor severity score reported for the 26 stroke patients, no explicit control group (3 healthy participants form a separate lab-validation cohort only).
