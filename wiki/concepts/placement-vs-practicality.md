## The tension, stated precisely

A lower back-mounted sensor still requires a more deliberate device placement than a pocket-carried smartphone, an extra step between a clinician's recommendation and a patient's actual daily use. [[shin-2022]] and [[lee-2025]], the only two included studies to test pocket placement directly in stroke participants, indicate this may be a tension of *degree* rather than *kind*: pocket placement appears usable, not absent, in a real stroke population, on considerably thinner evidence than the trunk-placement side of this comparison. What it costs specifically, relative to trunk, is not a quantity this review can currently size, since Shin's and Lee's own metrics are not comparable to each other or to the trunk-placement studies.

## The evidence, and its gap

[[pohl-2022]], [[hsu-2018]], and [[brasiliano-2026]] provide stroke-specific, systematic multi-placement comparisons. [[shin-2022]] and [[lee-2025]] separately provide stroke-specific, pocket-carried evidence. **No included study combines both** — no one tests a pocket-carried or unmodified consumer-smartphone position within the same multi-placement comparison against trunk and distal placements, in the same stroke participants. This is the review's single narrowest, most specific open gap — see [[future-directions]].

## A forward-looking reframe, not a recommendation

The trunk-vs-pocket choice may not need to resolve into a single winner. One possibility, **not yet tested by any included study**: a **tiered deployment** — a trunk/lumbar sensor for periodic, clinician-supervised assessment where accuracy is paramount, paired with a pocket-carried device for continuous, low-burden monitoring between visits where trend detection may matter more than absolute precision. Whether such a design would let each placement do the job its accuracy profile actually supports is itself an empirical question, not a settled conclusion this review is putting forward. This is explicitly framed as a hypothesis worth testing, not an empirical finding or a design recommendation.
