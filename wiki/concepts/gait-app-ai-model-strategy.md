# GaitTrace AI model strategy: measure recovery before classifying disease

Research synthesis as of 2026-10-01. This is a model-development recommendation, not a validated clinical claim or a report of a newly trained model. Product scope: independently ambulatory people after stroke, assessed with a horizontally held lower-back Android phone and, for clinical batteries, a worker-measured course and outcome form. See [[gait-app-collection-workflow]], [[classification-project-status]], and [the protocol specification](../../docs/REHAB_ASSESSMENT_PRODUCT_SPEC.md).

## Current milestone: 4 October supervisor review

The next milestone is a presentable supervised research collector, followed by validated feature extraction, clinically parameterized healthy-to-stroke-like synthesis, model training and later personalization. Autonomous clinical distance and few-shot adaptation should not block collector readiness. This preserves the future independent home-rehabilitation goal; it does not establish that the current app is a clinical outcome instrument.

Structured clinical intake, participant/session linkage and raw sensor exports are implemented. Reference-video synchronization and clinician-labelled event accuracy are not established by the demo. Before the next collection event, verify consented paired video, coded participant/session IDs, a clinician-agreed course variant, mounting/aid metadata, measured clock alignment and annotations for boundaries, walking, turns, rests and handling. Record unknown/unobservable clinical labels explicitly.

The integration is Google's on-device FitnessLocal Recording API, not Maps Directions. API access succeeded, but the first measured 3 m trial returned no records. The published 10 m out-and-back engineering option has no returned physical result yet. There is no verified 10 m minimum threshold. Keep this experiment separate from clinical outcomes. [Official Recording API guide](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/recording-api).

Retain all available raw sensors, including gyroscope, with native timestamps and explicit missingness; compare subsets empirically. G.A.I.T ratings require clinical observation and are not all measurable by one lumbar phone. Patient confidence needs an appropriate patient-reported reference; acceleration amplitude is not muscular strength. Voice commands may annotate events, but speech latency/missed recognition and patient speech difficulties prevent assuming exact floor crossings.

Validate deterministic quality/cadence/regularity features against paired references before using them as synthesis conditions. Repeated personal baselines and descriptive tracking can precede AI. Few-shot learning needs a trained base model and should outperform simple calibration on held-out future visits. No new model, acquisition, feature validation or clinical evaluation occurred in this review.

## Decision

The first deployable model target should be **quality-gated, interpretable within-person gait measurement**: identify usable straight-walking bouts, estimate cadence/step timing and trunk movement regularity, then describe changes between comparable visits alongside clinician-measured 10MWT speed, TUG time, and 2MWT/6MWT distance. It should abstain when placement, sampling, walking duration, or protocol context are inadequate. A single lower-back phone cannot establish neurological etiology or reliably assign paretic versus non-paretic foot events, measure foot clearance, or obtain clinical distance from IMU double integration. The existing stroke-vs-healthy model is a research benchmark, not an app diagnosis engine.

The grant's distinct primary objective remains **clinically parameterized healthy-to-stroke-like gait synthesis**. Its physics and cycle-contract pilot is an engineering component; it has generated healthy proxy IMU signals, not stroke gait. Synthesis can later support controlled sensitivity and robustness experiments, but generated trials cannot substitute for an independent real stroke cohort.

## Evidence and implications

| Evidence | Implication for this project |
| --- | --- |
| Buckley et al. tested a single lower-back accelerometer in 25 people after stroke against GAITRite. Vertical step regularity had the strongest reported association with reference asymmetry (Spearman rho 0.87) and test-retest ICC 0.98. [Original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6983246/) | Prioritize vertical step regularity as a **trunk-pattern proxy** to validate on the actual phone/holder. Do not label it left/right step-length asymmetry or assume responsiveness to rehabilitation; the authors explicitly call for that further work. |
| Del Din et al. evaluated a single lower-back accelerometer in 25 stroke participants. Step count validity was good, but agreement for several micro gait characteristics was only moderate/good or poor. [Original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5747176/) | Validate each proposed feature separately. A valid step count does not validate every timing or symmetry metric. |
| A 2025 smartphone study recorded at 50 Hz in a pocket and tested 20 healthy adults and 13 analysed stroke outpatients against heel IMUs. [Original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12299516/) | 50 Hz is a plausible starting rate for coarse gait events, but this is **not** proof for this lower-back holder, all phone models, detailed foot events, or 100 Hz equivalence. Retain actual timestamps/rates, inspect gaps and compare on paired reference trials. |
| A 2026 exploratory acute-stroke phone study used an L3 phone but manually segmented walking by video and used video for walking speed and foot stance. It had 22 acute stroke cases and 9 healthy controls. [Original study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12883-026-05057-3) | Do not treat published phone-derived classification or video-assisted gait labels as proof that this app autonomously measures those targets in a chronic cohort. |
| Nine chronic stroke participants could make step lengths more symmetrical while kinematic/kinetic asymmetry and impaired paretic propulsion persisted. [Original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7397591/) | Never make a single symmetry score the definition of recovery. Read it with speed, endurance, pauses, variability, assistive device and clinical examination. |
| Mobilise-D validated lower-back mobility estimation against a richer foot/insole reference system across several non-stroke cohorts and released a technical validation manual. [Original speed study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-51766-5), [technical manual](https://zenodo.org/records/14901135) | Borrow the modular validation design, reference synchronization and error reporting, not its numerical performance for this phone or stroke population. |
| Musculoskeletal simulations have tested how unilateral activation deficits change feasible walking mechanics, and synchronized motion-capture/physical-IMU work has shown how virtual sensor signals can be validated. [Impairment simulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3625686/), [virtual-IMU validation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10132030/) | Use a coupled motion/contact model and paired physical IMU checks for synthesis. These studies support the method, not a ready-made stroke parameter distribution or verified GaitTrace generator. |
| 10MWT measures marked-zone speed, TUG includes chair/turn transitions, and 2MWT/6MWT measure distance within fixed time. [10MWT](https://www.sralab.org/rehabilitation-measures/10-meter-walk-test), [TUG](https://www.sralab.org/rehabilitation-measures/timed-and-go), [2MWT](https://www.sralab.org/rehabilitation-measures/2-minute-walk-test), [6MWT](https://www.sralab.org/rehabilitation-measures/6-minute-walk-test) | Keep clinical outcomes from measured course/worker events separate from phone-estimated movement descriptors and require protocol/aid/course comparability for trends. |
| FDA digital-measure guidance distinguishes device verification, analytical validation, clinical validity, usability and intended use. [FDA paper](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/key-considerations-development-and-use-digitally-derived-measures-clinical-investigations-fda-paper) | An attractive dashboard and a high development AUC cannot establish that a measure is fit to guide rehabilitation. |

## Proposed model stack

1. **Measurement contract and quality gate.** Preserve native timestamps and three tri-axis streams, units, device/app/algorithm versions, protocol, physical orientation, holder type, aid, and static posture reference. Detect missing channels, timestamp gaps, saturation, poor attachment, abrupt shifts, non-walking, turns and insufficient straight steps. Magnetometer is optional for exploratory orientation and should not be required for gait scoring; indoor heading may be distorted. Record abstention explicitly, not as a healthy result. This is initially deterministic, with any later learned QC trained from labelled failure modes.
2. **Activity/event model.** Segment standing, straight walking, turning, rest and TUG transitions with a transparent signal-processing baseline. Train a small temporal model only if it demonstrably improves patient-held-out detection over that baseline. Annotate a video/reference subset; the app's current candidate peaks and software Go callback are not reference events.
3. **Feature estimator.** Derive cadence, step/stride interval distribution, vertical step/stride regularity, trunk acceleration/rotation amplitude and jerk, turn duration, pause burden, and early-versus-late endurance change. Reorient into a gravity/body frame only when its heading and placement are credible; otherwise prefer rotation-tolerant magnitudes but acknowledge loss of anatomical direction. Use measured course timing for 10MWT speed; keep heuristic step-distance/GPS separate and labelled experimental.
4. **Longitudinal interpretation.** Use a small hierarchical/mixed-effects or Bayesian within-person model after repeatability is measured. Compare same participant, protocol, speed condition, course, aid and attachment. Report absolute clinical outcomes, sensor features, uncertainty and whether change exceeds empirically measured error; avoid a single health score. Generate multilingual voice/text feedback from vetted evidence-to-phrase rules, not an unconstrained diagnostic language model. During standardized timed batteries, preserve protocol-consistent cueing.
5. **Research synthesis.** Continue the existing contact/cadence/speed/paretic-side parameter contract and virtual IMU with a coherent musculoskeletal or motion representation, validated lower-back attachment, realistic device sampling/holder artifacts, and clinician-reviewed parameter ranges. Preserve both directions of spatial asymmetry; do not encode every stroke patient as one stereotyped gait. Condition on measured movement variables and keep diagnosis/severity metadata distinct. Compare generated kinematics, kinetics, foot contacts and phone IMU against real paired reference data. Lock parent-disjoint roles before fitting; synthetic data never enter the final real-patient test set.
6. **Later discriminative model, if needed.** A scoped task might predict *clinician-reviewed gait pattern requiring assessment* or a validated functional outcome, with an explicit indeterminate result. It must be tested against age-matched healthy participants and hard negatives (Parkinsonism, orthopedic conditions, cane effects, low sleep/attention where ethically and safely observable), across phones/sites/holders and with participant-disjoint final validation. It must not claim to distinguish stroke from alcohol, sleep deprivation or another neurological cause from gait alone.

## Local research status and admission gates

- Frozen `v0.2.0` is a 15-member, 100 Hz, 500-sample, lower-back acceleration-magnitude healthy-versus-stroke classifier. It is not clinically validated or directly compatible with the app's variable-rate, horizontal-phone protocol. In local development stress, 89/138 non-stroke Voisard participants were positive; TVS produced 17/18 healthy and 16/16 Parkinson positive calls. The completed three-class and feature experiments did not solve specificity. [Evidence map](../../docs/CLASSIFICATION_EVIDENCE_MAP.md), [integration plan](../../docs/STROKE_CLASSIFICATION_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md). RevalExo is historical evidence only, not a clean final test or tuning source.
- The synthesis contract and six-channel virtual-IMU numerical pilot have passed engineering checks on healthy GAITEX pelvis-proxy signals, but no stroke-like motion transformation, verified L5 attachment, clinician-backed parameter distribution or independent clinical evaluation exists. [Synthesis specification](../../docs/classification/STROKE_SYNTHESIS_PARAMETER_SPEC.md).
- Before any app claim: (a) paired phone + independent reference on the actual holder and target population; (b) participant- and site/device-disjoint validation; (c) per-feature bias/error/repeatability, subgroup and failure-mode reporting; (d) longitudinal responsiveness against clinician outcomes; (e) locked independent final cohort never used to choose models or thresholds. A 50 Hz analysis grid may standardize feature extraction, but never invent 100 Hz information or erase timing gaps.

## Next bounded experiment

With a rehabilitation partner, prioritize repeat 10MWT and TUG sessions from independently ambulatory chronic stroke participants and matched controls, including cane strata; use 2MWT as secondary endurance assessment. Retain 6MWT historical compatibility without making it a default collection requirement. Pair the horizontal lower-back phone with video/instrumented walkway or bilateral foot references for a subset, and worker-measured outcomes for every trial. Freeze attachment and protocol definitions, annotate turns/rest/looseness, and compare a deterministic QC + vertical regularity/cadence baseline to any learned model using participant-disjoint development. Only promote a feature whose error and repeatability support the exact patient-facing claim. This is a proposed study, not completed acquisition.

### Corrections to the proposed action plan

1. **Profile and eligibility:** subcortical is a lesion location, not a hemisphere alternative. Record hemisphere and affected body side separately. Use an onset date or approximate chronicity with precision/source, rather than inventing a date. Walker use expands the earlier none/single-point/quad-cane inclusion scope and needs clinical confirmation; aid type alone does not establish independence.
2. **10MWT variant:** the [AbilityLab instrument sheet](https://www.sralab.org/sites/default/files/2017-06/10%20Meter%20Walk%20Test%20Instructions.pdf) uses 10 m total, central 6 m timed and three trials per condition. Its [summary](https://www.sralab.org/rehabilitation-measures/10-meter-walk-test) describes other variants and two trials per condition. Select one clinician-approved version, not a mixture. Central 10 m plus two 2 m zones requires 14 m total. Speed must use the actual timed distance, not a fixed 10 m numerator after changing to 6 m.
3. **Endurance/boundaries:** [2MWT](https://www.sralab.org/rehabilitation-measures/2-minute-walk-test) measures distance in two minutes, not timer-only performance. [TUG](https://www.sralab.org/rehabilitation-measures/timed-and-go) is Go-to-seated timing. Recognized Stop is not verified chair contact or foot crossing. Store audio/command/detection times separately from observer reference boundaries and quantify timing bias.
4. **Observability:** gravity defines vertical, not forward heading, anatomical placement, center of mass or true coronal displacement. Keep mounting confirmation and phone lean separate from biological trunk lean. Personal zeroing must not delete original posture from records. [Lumbar stroke evidence](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6983246/) supports regularity as a candidate trunk-pattern proxy, not all bilateral stance, step-length or clearance metrics.
5. **Speech feasibility:** Android's explicit on-device recognizer starts at [API 31](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/speech/SpeechRecognizer); the user's Android 11 Redmi cannot be promised that route. Evaluate a compatible service or bundled offline command engine in airplane mode, English/Malay/Chinese, lower-back placement and noise; test speech impairment and TTS self-triggering. New native recognition/permissions require a [new binary](https://docs.expo.dev/eas-update/introduction/). Keep microphone audio transient unless separate recording consent/retention is established.
6. **API spending:** [Google migration guidance](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/migration/fit) separates health records from real-time sensors/location; [HealthKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit) is a repository. The free-provider audit found no documented clinically calibrated solution for the confirmed screen-out lumbar mount. ARCore front-camera world tracking is unavailable. A custom OpenVINS front-camera/IMU prototype is the free feasibility candidate; the inspected Android port uses the rear camera and is not a verified replacement. Device calibration, native acquisition and paired validation precede any clinical claim. See the [provider audit](../../docs/REHAB_ASSESSMENT_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#free-distance-provider-feasibility-audit--2-october-2026).
7. **Personalization:** historical best alone biases comparisons; preserve repeated initial baseline, recent comparable reference and absolute outcomes. [MetaGait](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-35121-4) demonstrates few-shot shank-IMU cycle generation/reconstruction, not validated lumbar-phone recovery scoring. It supports research plausibility, not immediate patient fine-tuning.

### Recommended execution order

1. **Measurement contract and focused app changes:** agree eligibility/course variant/trial count/reference boundaries with the clinician; structure intake, prioritize menus and version exports; extend natural-standing calibration without a phone-setup deadline. Keep standardized clinical cues separate from practice encouragement. Confidence is a patient-reported construct, e.g. clinically suitable [ABC](https://www.sralab.org/rehabilitation-measures/activities-specific-balance-confidence-scale), and independence a worker-assessed construct, e.g. [FAC](https://www.sralab.org/rehabilitation-measures/functional-ambulation-category); neither is established by smooth phone motion. Check language/instrument suitability before adoption.
2. **Paired pilot and speech feasibility:** repeat marked-course/stopwatch trials with synchronized reference on the actual holder. Label foot events, turns, pauses and attachment faults on a subset. Predefine per-feature acceptable error with the clinician; measure speech latency and false/missed activations by device/language. Retain worker timing. A feasibility pilot is not independent clinical validation.
3. **Modular estimators:** QC/phase detection first; measured-distance speed arithmetic and validated cadence next; trunk regularity/rotation and TUG phase durations only where reference labels support them. Shared features and small task heads are sufficient; three separate neural networks are not mandatory. Store unreliable/absent outcomes explicitly.
4. **Longitudinal adaptation later:** measure repeatability; compare fixed estimation plus simple calibration against few-shot adaptation. Use early visits as support, later visits as chronological held-out queries and participant-disjoint population/meta-training. Avoid future-session leakage and re-zeroing that conceals decline. Promote adaptation only if independent accuracy improves over the simple baseline.

This feedback agrees with the prior functional app-model strategy. It does **not explicitly cancel** the earlier clinically parameterized healthy-to-stroke-like synthesis grant objective; confirm scope before replacing it. Keep synthesis and functional-measure validation distinct meanwhile. Wiley status is user-reported; no submission access or response deadline was verified.

## Autonomous phone measurement correction (2 October 2026)

The local patient path now records hands-free, estimates distance/speed after capture and completes automatically. Worker stopwatch and entered outcomes remain an optional development reference route. A single horizontal lumbar phone observes movement but not exact floor-mark crossing or chair contact; the current 10MWT/TUG quiet-stop is provisional and can mistake a long rest for completion. Candidate walking/turn/sit events and a personal distance model need paired validation on this holder and target population. Do not label inferred boundaries or height-times-steps as verified clinical measurements. Keep calibration versioned and independent of the current test, and allow an unavailable estimate when quality or inputs are inadequate. ## Camera feasibility decision update - 4 October 2026

The user permits camera use and consideration of changing the mounting method to expose the rear camera. Recommend retaining the horizontal lower-back anatomical location, with a snug holder that exposes the rear lens and keeps it clear of clothing; confirm actual optical view and comfort before patient testing. ARCore front-camera sessions have paused world tracking and identity display-oriented pose, so face tracking is not a distance substitute. [Official camera configuration contract](https://developers.google.com/ar/reference/java/com/google/ar/core/CameraConfig.FacingDirection). Redmi Note 10 Pro is listed as supported, but runtime availability must be checked on the actual handset.

Preferred feasibility route: rear-camera ARCore, local processing, separate experiment, measured 3/5/10 m paths including turns, explicit tracking-loss flags and reference timing. Test filtered horizontal path distance rather than raw 3D position increments, which can accumulate trunk sway/jitter; a return to the starting point must not report zero travelled distance. Camera motion estimates do not automatically identify clinical tape crossings or supply calibrated 10MWT outcomes. Do not silently replace lost visual tracking with heuristic distance.

If the front camera must remain outward, a custom calibrated visual-inertial estimator such as OpenVINS is a research candidate, not verified phone support or a plug-in service. A calibrated known-size printed-marker setup is another controlled-course candidate; its range/visibility must be validated and a single endpoint marker does not provide continuous path length. No camera route was implemented or tested in this review. Native integration would require a new APK and paired validation; changing the mount also requires orientation/frame metadata so opposite-facing raw axes are not mixed with earlier captures.
